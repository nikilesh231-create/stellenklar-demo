Product thinking

What StellenKlar is solving

A lightweight decision layer for job applications: understand a role, compare it with proven evidence, surface blockers early and make the next action explicit.

1. Evidence profile

Create a reusable profile from a master CV, work authorization, languages and target roles.

2. Job analysis

Paste a posting and detect role, location, language and core requirements before matching.

3. Decision

Separate supported strengths, practical risks and genuine gaps, then suggest the next move.
